Circle enumeration and a sharpness family for resonant Rossby wave triads

Peiwei Wu and Qian Peng

Read the paper (PDF) Lean theorem audit Source repository

Abstract

For the square-torus Rossby dispersion relation at infinite deformation radius, we give a two-square enumeration of the exact resonant triads through a prescribed nonzonal mode. If this mode has squared length M, there are at most 2r2(M3) ordered decompositions. For a box of side parameter N, the total number of circle points examined is O(N2(1 + log N)7), giving deterministic enumeration in N2(log N)O(1) time. We also construct primitive triads whose positive output components (a, b) satisfy (a/b4)2 = 3 − 8/b2 for unbounded b. Along this family a/b4 approaches √3 from below, so any uniform polynomial upper bound for a in terms of b must have exponent at least four. Subsequent counterexamples exceed Kopp's proposed coefficient √3.

Relation to Kopp’s work

The Pell family establishes a lower bound on the exponent in a possible uniform bound. A separate paper on Kopp's Conjecture 5 gives the counterexamples to the proposed coefficient. Kopp’s original article (2017).
