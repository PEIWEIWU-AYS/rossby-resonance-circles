# Prior work and public search

28 November 2026, Melbourne. This check covers exact integer triads on the square beta-plane torus in the infinite-deformation-radius limit. We searched separately for a fixed-mode circle algorithm and for a primitive family approaching the proposed quartic anisotropy bound.

## Closest papers

- Bustamante and Hayat classified the integer triads through rational points and representations by quadratic forms. Kishimoto and Yoneda studied the arithmetic and anisotropy of the wavenumber set. [Bustamante and Hayat](https://arxiv.org/search/?query=Complete+classification+of+discrete+resonant+Rossby&searchtype=title) · [Kishimoto and Yoneda](https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.1031)
- Harris, Connaughton and Bustamante studied the real quartic resonance and quasi-resonance curves for a prescribed mode using a pair of quadratic surfaces. The integer circle used here has squared radius equal to the cube of the output norm squared. Its integer points give candidates for two Gaussian-integer roots. [Harris, Connaughton and Bustamante](https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/15/8/083011)
- Kopp gave the rational parametrization, the elliptic-curve fibration and the conjectured inequality for the horizontal and vertical components. Our Pell specialization uses that parametrization. The resulting primitive solutions approach the proposed coefficient from below. Subsequent [counterexamples](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24591008) show that some primitive resonances exceed the proposed coefficient. [Kopp, published paper](https://doi.org/10.1137/16M1077593) · [Author's PDF, Conjecture 5](https://gskopp.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/rossbytriads_kopp.pdf)
- Hayat, Amanullah, Walsh and Bustamante gave a complete direct search with cubic complexity in the box side. A later paper applied a search over sampled parameter grids. We found neither the fixed-mode two-to-one integer-circle bound nor the aggregate divisor estimate establishing a near-quadratic complete search in these papers. [Cubic search](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.03092) · [Later enumeration](https://doi.org/10.3390/math10234395)
