# Circle enumeration and a sharpness family for resonant Rossby wave triads | 共振罗斯贝波三元组的整数圆枚举与四次增长构造

Exact Rossby wave triads on the square torus: near-quadratic enumeration by integer circles and a Pell family proving a quartic growth lower bound. Preprint and code.

本文把给定输出波向量的共振分解化为整数圆上的枚举，得到近二次时间的有限盒完整搜索。另一组 Pell 方程构造说明，统一多项式上界的指数至少为四。

## Original question and result

[Gene S. Kopp, *The Arithmetic Geometry of Resonant Rossby Wave Triads*, §§1.7–1.8 (2017)](https://doi.org/10.1137/16M1077593). The circle construction enumerates every fully nonzonal triad in a finite box. The Pell family separately proves a lower limit on any uniform growth exponent. The counterexamples to the coefficient in Kopp's Conjecture 5 appear in a [separate paper](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24591008).
